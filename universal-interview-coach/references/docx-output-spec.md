# DOCX output specification

Use this reference when generating a new interview-preparation DOCX.

## Fixed content order

1. 岗位基础信息
2. 企业概况与岗位阶段分析
3. 基础知识、名称与缩写解释
4. 适配岗位的自我介绍
5. 高频问题、案例题与答案

## Recommended components

- opening summary: one-page role verdict and evidence boundary;
- definition list or compact table: role metadata;
- prose and short bullets: company and role analysis;
- knowledge definition list: term, expansion, explanation, use, trap, wording;
- three answer blocks: 30-second, 1-minute, and 2-minute self-introduction when useful;
- question table: question, intent, priority, source confidence;
- answer blocks: recommended structure, personalized answer, follow-ups, risk note;
- final checklist: knowledge gaps, facts to confirm, and practice order.

## Evidence labels

Use these labels consistently:

- `已核验`
- `用户确认`
- `可迁移`
- `课程学习`
- `正在补强`
- `岗位推断`
- `待确认`

## Filename

`公司_岗位_面试准备_YYYY-MM-DD.docx`

Use `公司待确认` when the employer cannot be established from the supplied material.
